# Monetization Model & Compliance Specification — JeetoIndian

## 1. Executive Summary & Legal Guardrails

**JeetoIndian** operates strictly on a **B2B Sponsor-Funded and Non-Entry-Fee Advertising Business Model**.

### Core Business Pillars
1. **100% Free Participation**: Users NEVER pay an entry fee, registration fee, deposit, stake, wager, paid attempt, or prize pool contribution to participate in any competition or win any prize.
2. **Zero Financial Instruments**: The platform explicitly DOES NOT feature cash wallets, deposit balances, cash-out mechanisms, paid retries, paid second chances, or user-to-user betting.
3. **Primary Revenue (Sponsorships)**: Corporate brands pay sponsorship/campaign fees to host sponsored challenges, receive brand engagement, and supply/fund physical or non-cash prizes.
4. **Secondary Revenue (Platform Monetization Ads)**: A free competition MAY present at most **ONE** platform monetization advertisement opportunity during the competition join flow.

## 2. Platform Monetization Ad Rules & Principles

```
  [User Taps JOIN FREE]
           │
           ▼
  [Server Validates Eligibility & Limits]
           │
           ▼
  [Optional/Available Monetization Ad Opportunity] ◄─── (Max 1 Ad per Competition Join)
           │
           ├── (Ad Completed)   ──► [Join Competition (FREE)]
           ├── (Ad Skipped)     ──► [Join Competition (FREE)]
           ├── (Ad Unavailable) ──► [Join Competition (FREE)]
           └── (Ad Failed)      ──► [Join Competition (FREE)]
           │
           ▼
  [Quiz Session Begins]
```

### Statutory Non-Negotiables
- **Monetization, NOT Consideration**: Viewing or completing an ad is platform monetization revenue. It is NEVER legal consideration or stake for entering a competition.
- **Zero Impact on Gameplay & Winning**: Viewing or completing an ad MUST NOT change the user's quiz score, tie-breaker ranking, eligibility, probability of winning, or prize value.
- **Non-Blocking Resilience**: If the ad provider fails, time outs, is unavailable, or is skipped where permitted, the user **STILL joins the competition for FREE**. The ad provider is never a blocking dependency for competition entry.
- **Strict Ad Frequency Limits**:
  - NO ads shown before individual quiz questions.
  - NO multiple ads during a single competition join flow.
  - NO mandatory "pay-to-skip" mechanisms.
